Boston Environmental Justice Issues

Environmental Justice combines comprehensive management of the environment, parks and recreation, as well as public works sectors of a given town. Unfortunately, members of minority groups are often negatively affected by these institutions, as they are given less focus than predominantly majority neighborhoods, which can heighten issues these groups already face. Visit: Boston.gov City Council website to learn more.
